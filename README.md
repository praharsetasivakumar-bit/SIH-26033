## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1. Proposed solution
A mobile and web platform connecting farmers and FPOs directly with retailers, processors, and other buyers. Farmers list their produce, compare offers, and arrange delivery through shared transport.
2. How it addresses the problem
By reducing unnecessary trading steps, improving price transparency, and helping farmers sell in larger FPO-aggregated lots, FarmLink can increase farmers’ share of the sale price and reduce avoidable costs.
3. Innovation and uniqueness
FarmLink combines buyer matching, price comparison, produce aggregation, logistics, and payment tracking in one platform. Local-language and SMS access help make it usable for farmers with limited internet access.

## Feasibility and Viability
FarmLink can be built using widely available mobile and web technologies and piloted through existing FPOs and collection centres. Starting in one crop cluster keeps setup and operating costs manageable. Revenue could come from a small, transparent transaction fee paid by buyers or service providers. The pilot should track farmer earnings, buyer prices, delivery costs, and repeat usage before expansion.

## Impact and Benefits
FarmLink can help farmers reach more buyers and earn more through transparent offers and lower avoidable costs. Consumers and retailers may benefit from better access to fresh produce, while FPO aggregation and shared transport can reduce waste and delivery costs.
